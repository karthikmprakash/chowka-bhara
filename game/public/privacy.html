Privacy policy

Your table stays yours.

Chowka Bara is a board game, not a data business. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and nothing sold to anyone. This page explains the little the game does store, why, and when it disappears.

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

  • No account, email address, phone number, or password.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising ID.
  • Online rooms store your chosen display name, your avatar, and the game state so the other players can see the table.
  • A room and everything in it is deleted automatically within 24 hours of being created.
  • Local and practice games never leave your device unless you choose to create or join an online room.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps a small amount of information in your browser or app storage so it remembers where you left off:

  • Your display name and chosen avatar.
  • Language, theme, sound, and beginner or advanced mode.
  • Your saved local or practice game and courtyard journey badges.
  • A random reconnect token for an online room, so a refresh or a dropped connection can rejoin the same seat.

This information stays on your device. You can remove all of it by clearing the app’s storage or your browser site data.

What an online room stores on our servers

Online play needs a shared table, so a room keeps the minimum needed to run the game:

  • The six-character room code.
  • Each player’s display name and avatar choice.
  • The game state: board size, whose turn it is, throws, pawn positions, and scores.
  • A random reconnect token for each seat, used only to prove that a returning device is the same player.

Rooms are hosted on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. Cloudflare processes network metadata such as your IP address to deliver the game and protect the service, as any hosting provider does. We do not build profiles from it.

How long it stays

  • Online rooms: deleted within 24 hours of the room being created. If every player leaves, the room is usually deleted about 15 minutes after the last player disconnects. Deleting a room removes its names, tokens, and game state.
  • Local data: stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Email: if you write to us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

We do not keep a separate database or backup copy of room data.

Who can see it

  • Other players at your table see your display name, avatar, and moves. That is the point of a shared board. Nobody outside the room can see it unless you share the room code.
  • Cloudflare hosts the game as our service provider and processes it on our behalf.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train anything.

What we never collect

No account or contact details, no contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files. No advertising ID. No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The Android app asks for only two permissions: internet access and vibration for the cowrie throw.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies for advertising or analytics. The game uses your browser or app storage for the preferences and saved game described above. That storage is strictly functional: without it, the game could not remember your language, your name, or your seat.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS with TLS. Room codes are random and hard to guess, and there are no passwords to steal. A reconnect token only grants access to one seat in one room, and it dies with the room. No system is perfect, so please do not put anything private in your display name.

Children

Chowka Bara is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child plays, the only information shared is the display name that was typed at the table. A parent or guardian can write to us to have a room deleted early.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to confirm what a room holds, correct a display name, or delete a room before its 24 hours are up. You can also object to processing or ask for a copy of what is stored. Write to mkarthikprakash.work@gmail.com with the room code if you have it. We reply within 30 days. Because there is no account, we may need the room code or approximate time of play to find the right table.

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the rights in the GDPR. If you are in India, you have the rights in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We honour these requests wherever you live.

Where your data is processed

Cloudflare runs the game on its global network, so a room may be processed outside your country. Cloudflare’s standard contractual clauses cover those transfers.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data changes, we update this page and its date. A material change will also be noted in the app. If you keep playing after a change, the new policy applies.

Contact

Chowka Bara is made by Karthik M Prakash, an independent developer. Questions, requests, and complaints go to mkarthikprakash.work@gmail.com. We will always answer.
